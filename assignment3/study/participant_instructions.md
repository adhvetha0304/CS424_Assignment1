# Assignment 3: Visualization Evaluation

## Participant Instructions

Thank you for taking part in this study!

This study compares two versions of a visualization showing how many people were observed in four shared spaces across seven sessions.

- **Static version:** A heatmap with a reference table showing the observation details.
- **Interactive version:** The same heatmap, with options to click cells and to highlight sessions based on space and noise level.

The goal is to compare how people use the two versions. There are no expectations about how well you should perform.

Please answer independently and as accurately as you can. I won't tell you whether an answer is correct until the study is finished. Participation is voluntary, and you can stop at any time.

## Before Starting

- Please use a laptop. If you can't, tell me before we start.
- Use the same device for all four questions.
- You can scroll through the page as needed.
- Tell me right away if anything isn't loading or displaying properly.
- Before the first question, I'll ask how familiar you are with charts (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
